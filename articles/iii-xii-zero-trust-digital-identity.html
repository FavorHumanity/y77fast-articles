Article III/XII · DIGITAL ID

Zero-Trust Digital Identity

Trust nothing by default. Verify everything.

A digital ID should pass three gates:

  1. Authenticity: the signature checks against the issuer's public key.
  2. Integrity: the hash matches, so nothing was changed.
  3. Revocation: revocation is checked against a signed list.
